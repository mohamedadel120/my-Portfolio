# Hero brief: muhammed-adel.web.app

Scope: the home page hero only, inside the existing Jaspr site (direction c).
Interviewed 25 Oct 2026. Answers that the owner chose are quoted; everything
else is marked as authored.

## Interview

1. **Vibe.** Owner: "يفضل هادي وبريميم" (stays calm and premium, direction c:
   light canvas #F6F6F3, ink, one teal #0E7490, Geist). References (authored):
   a Kinfolk product spread, Apple's product-page photography, morning light on
   a desk.
2. **Journey.** Fixed by the site: hero, then Projects, Experience, About,
   Contact. The hero's job is the first two beats: who this is, then proof.
3. **Energy.** Calm open, one lift, calm hand-off into Projects (authored).
4. **Feeling and the one moment.** Owner: "وريني التلاته عشان محتار" (show all
   three). Three prototypes, one per candidate moment:
   - A. The apps rise out of the headline: phones pass in front of the title
     and spread apart while you scroll, then settle toward Projects.
   - B. One phone comes closer until its screen fills the view.
   - C. Calm depth only: planes drift at different rates, nothing else moves.
5. **The one thing no site does.** Authored, pending the chosen moment: the
   real app screens sit inside a photographed scene, so the proof is the
   picture itself.
6. **Range.** Premium-minimal, the owner's pick.
7. **One world or scenes.** One scene (hero only).
8. **Assets.** Owner chose a generated photographic scene ("مشهد فوتوغرافي
   متولّد"), via kie.ai. Real app screenshots (Gomla, Stock, Albatal) go on the
   phone screens as markup; no app UI is generated.

Scroll: owner chose "من غير تثبيت خالص" (no pinning at all). All motion runs
while the page scrolls normally; the hero never holds the scroll.

## Hard rules for this hero

- Only confirmed facts in copy: Flutter developer, 3+ years, Cairo, Egypt,
  Android and iOS, Gomla and Stock 10,000+ downloads each, Albatal 1,000+.
- No text baked into images. The headline and buttons stay real HTML.
- No scroll cue, no counters, no em dash, no glow, no gradient text.
- Reduced motion: the same composition, still. No hidden content.
- Mobile gets its own crop and layer order.

## Feeling curve (hero)

| Moment | Feeling | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| Open | Calm, "this is careful work" | Morning light, one clear headline, the phones already in the scene |
| First 40% of the scroll | Curiosity, the scene has depth | Planes separate at different rates |
| Peak (depends on A/B/C) | "Oh, these are real apps" | The real screens come forward |
| Hand-off | Settled, ready to look | The scene clears into Projects |

**Peak:** chosen after the three prototypes. Sentence to fill in: "It's the
site where ___."

## Authored silence

None. The hero is short and never pins.
