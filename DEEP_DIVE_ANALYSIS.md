## 1. CHD ART MAKER (https://www.chdartmaker.com/)

### **Visual Design System**

#### Typography Hierarchy
- **Hero Title**: Extremely large (120-150px), bold, sans-serif
- **Section Titles**: Large (64-80px), bold, uppercase or title case
- **Body Text**: Medium (16-18px), regular weight, high line-height (1.6-1.8)
- **Meta Text**: Small (12-14px), lighter weight, subtle color
- **Font Family**: Clean sans-serif (likely custom or premium font)
- **Letter Spacing**: Tight for large text (-1 to -2px), normal for body

#### Color Palette
- **Primary Background**: Deep dark (#0A0E27 or similar)
- **Text Primary**: Pure white or near-white (#FFFFFF)
- **Text Secondary**: Light gray (#CCCCCC or rgba)
- **Accent Colors**: Minimal, used sparingly
- **Gradients**: Subtle, dark-to-darker transitions

#### Layout Structure
- **Grid System**: 12-column grid with generous gutters
- **Container Width**: Max-width ~1400px, centered
- **Padding**: Large horizontal padding (80-120px desktop), responsive
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Consistent spacing (40px, 60px, 80px, 100px)
- **White Space**: Generous, breathing room between sections

#### Visual Elements
- **Images**: Large, high-quality, full-width or large cards
- **Borders**: Thin (1px), subtle colors, rounded corners (8-12px)
- **Shadows**: Soft, subtle, minimal elevation
- **Icons**: Minimalist, line-style, consistent stroke width

### **Interaction Patterns**

#### Scroll Behavior
- **Smooth Scrolling**: Buttery smooth, likely using Locomotive Scroll or similar
- **Parallax Effects**: Subtle, background elements move slower
- **Scroll Triggers**: Animations trigger at specific scroll positions
- **Scroll Progress**: Visual indicator (top bar or side indicator)

#### Hover States
- **Project Cards**: Subtle scale (1.02-1.05), shadow increase, border highlight
- **Buttons**: Background color change, slight lift
- **Links**: Underline animation, color transition
- **Images**: Subtle zoom (1.1x), overlay on hover

#### Animations
- **Page Load**: Fade-in for sections, stagger for cards
- **Scroll Animations**: Fade-in, slide-up, scale-in
- **Micro-interactions**: Button press feedback, form field focus
- **Transitions**: Smooth (300-600ms), ease-out curves

### **Content Strategy**

#### Information Architecture
1. **Hero**: Large title, brief description, CTA
2. **About/Introduction**: Story, mission, values
3. **Services/Expertise**: 4-5 key services in grid
4. **Projects**: Featured projects, "Découvrir" buttons
5. **Clients**: Logo grid or list
6. **Contact**: Form, contact info, social links

#### Copywriting Style
- **Tone**: Professional, sophisticated, minimal
- **Length**: Short, impactful sentences
- **Language**: French/English, formal but approachable
- **CTAs**: "Découvrir" (Discover), "En savoir plus" (Learn more)

### **Technical Insights**

#### Performance
- **Image Optimization**: WebP format, lazy loading
- **Code Splitting**: Likely using modern bundling
- **Animation Performance**: GPU-accelerated transforms
- **Loading Strategy**: Progressive enhancement

#### Accessibility
- **Color Contrast**: High contrast ratios (WCAG AA+)
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Full keyboard support
- **Screen Readers**: Semantic HTML, ARIA labels
- **Focus States**: Visible focus indicators

## 🎨 **Common Design Patterns Across All Websites**

### **1. Typography**
- **Large Headlines**: 80-150px for hero text
- **Bold Weight**: Heavy (700-900) for impact
- **Split Text**: Multiple lines for visual interest
- **Tight Spacing**: Negative letter-spacing for large text
- **High Contrast**: White on dark, dark on light

### **2. Layout**
- **Generous White Space**: Breathing room between elements
- **Full-Width Sections**: Immersive, no constraints
- **Centered Content**: Max-width containers, centered
- **Consistent Spacing**: 40px, 60px, 80px, 100px rhythm
- **Responsive Grid**: Flexible, mobile-first

### **3. Color**
- **Dark Backgrounds**: Deep blacks, dark grays
- **High Contrast**: White text, clear readability
- **Minimal Accents**: Single or few accent colors
- **Subtle Gradients**: Dark-to-darker transitions
- **Strategic Use**: Color for emphasis, not decoration

### **4. Animations**
- **Smooth Scrolling**: Locomotive Scroll or similar
- **Scroll Triggers**: Animations on scroll position
- **Stagger Effects**: Sequential animations
- **Parallax**: Multiple layers, different speeds
- **Micro-interactions**: Button feedback, hovers

### **5. Content**
- **Short Copy**: Concise, impactful
- **Clear CTAs**: Prominent, action-oriented
- **Visual First**: Images over text
- **Storytelling**: Narrative approach
- **Professional Tone**: Confident, modern
